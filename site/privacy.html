Privacy

cfctl telemetry is off by default. The CLI stores profiles, plans, catalogs, and redacted evidence locally. OAuth credentials remain in the operating system credential store. The cfctl.io callback page processes the authorization response only in your browser, does not send it to cfctl.io, and lets you copy it to the CLI.

The project website does not require an account. Hosting providers may retain ordinary request logs under their own policies.
